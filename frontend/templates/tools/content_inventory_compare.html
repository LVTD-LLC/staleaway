Compare your three URL lists

Start with the clearly fictional example.com lists, or replace all three. Paste one URL per line, not CSV or XML. Use public page URLs only: never paste private URLs, review-link tokens or credentials. No inputs or results are uploaded, saved, added to the address bar or included in analytics.

Each list requires 1–2,000 nonblank lines, at most 250,000 bytes and 2,048 characters per URL. Only http/https URLs without queries or fragments are supported. Local/private hosts and IPv6 literals are not supported. No network fetch verifies whether a hostname is public or a page exists.

Your workload estimate

Assumes distinct pages, equal review time and every session used. Spare minutes do not carry between sessions. Reserved time includes the full final session; page slots may exceed your review list. Fixes, research and interruptions may need additional time.

This is not an audit score, a deadline or a forecast of Staleaway coverage. It does not change your reminder settings.

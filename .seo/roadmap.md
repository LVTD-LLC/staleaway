# SEO roadmap

1. Public discovery and publication boundaries: robots sitemap discovery, links to existing public pages, accurate metadata, published-only blog views, robust article schema. Current run.
2. Develop an original website content review checklist and cadence guide: inspect public demand, validate workflow examples, choose one intent owner and link from homepage plus blog. Do not fabricate keyword volume.
3. Verify acquisition-to-signup and first-reminder measurement without private page URLs in analytics. Existing live event definitions do not establish successful activation tracking.
4. Compare simple reminder workflows with automated monitoring only after demand research; do not position Staleaway as feature-equivalent.
5. Recheck search outcomes after 21/28 days; ordinary new-domain indexing lag is not a publishing veto.

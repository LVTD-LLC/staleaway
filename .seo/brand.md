# Staleaway brand

Staleaway is a free website content review reminder app for website owners who need a repeatable human review habit. Add a sitemap, choose a schedule and receive a few pages by email. Its advantage is a small, focused workflow, not automatic auditing or rewriting.

Source of positioning: VISION.md and PRODUCT.md, checked 2026-10-09. Not for automated SEO audits, content generation, agency billing or agent APIs. All features are currently free; do not imply permanence.

Voice inferred from current homepage: concise, warm, practical, gentle reminders rather than fear. Short sentences; concrete page examples; no ranking guarantees, hype or invented usage statistics. Preserve the Firefox-inspired design. No new author credentials or first-party aggregate data may be invented. Private customer pages, email addresses and records are off-limits for public content.
